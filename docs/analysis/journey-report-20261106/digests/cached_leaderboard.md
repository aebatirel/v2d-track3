The arc had four phases.
1. **First submission and reference rebuild (24–26 Oct).** Pack 1 scored AUC 0.57468, against a projection of 0.68. Pack 2 fixed eval-30 detection and picked seed-43 policies (+0.008). Pack 3 rebuilt references with the track guard, added the standard learner, the eval-25 board identity fix and the eval-20 arc foot. That one pack moved AUC +0.206 to 0.78893, 4th on AUC. Collision carves in pack 4 gave +0.0216, and the field then passed us (DDD 0.827).
2. **Small policy swaps (26 Oct to 1 Nov).** Swaps of ±0.001 could not keep pace while the field rose 0.03–0.10, and AUC fell to 7th–8th. The 31 Oct standings check exposed CD-O (18th of 20) as the anchor of a rank sum of 62. Mesh-only changes then became the cheapest rank points (9a+: CD-O 0.40188 → 0.33234).
3. **Eval 10 (1 Nov).** A contact-overflow root cause was found, and the njmax-1024-trained policy in pack 11 added +0.020 AUC.
4. **Meshes (2–3 Nov).** Reading the kit scorer showed that our submitted mesh is ICP-registered to the hidden scan, so mesh shape and placement drive every pose board. The Tier B meshes in pack 12e gave +0.029 AUC over 12d, −0.62 cm RPE, −0.48 cm MPPE, plus one SP-SR and one MP-SR episode each, taking the rank sum from 54 to 47. The pose-CV meshes in 13p added +0.010 AUC. CD-O-only variants (13h, 13i, 13j) built from generated meshes, selected best-of-N against public scans (GT-informed, owner-approved, disclosed), took CD-O to 4th.

### learnings_task
- The final rank is the sum of the six board ranks (FAQ). An early research note (6 Oct) assumed 'AUC likely sole ranking metric', and the first rank-sum check came only on 31 Oct. That check showed CD-O at 18th of 20 anchoring a rank sum of 62 (11th). From then on, mesh-only CD-O work was the cheapest source of rank points: CD-O went 18 → 14 → 11 → 8 → 6 → 5 → 4.
- Each Kaggle board shows a team's best submission. The counted one is the selected Final Submission, else the best per board (PR #215 kit CONTAINER_SUBMISSION.md). So CD-O-only variants (12d, 13h, 13i, 13j) could be uploaded without risk to the pose boards, and the final plan selects per board: 14p for five boards, 13j for CD-O.
- Kaggle scores the submitted trajectories as they are; nothing is re-run (kit AUC.py). Lucky draws are therefore banked: pack 6 packed 0.9164 against a 10-draw mean of 0.8755, and a swap to a more robust policy is worth only about +0.0003 to +0.0007 on the board. PR #215 makes results provisional until a container reproduces every counted file, so stable checkpoints and exact provenance still matter.
- The kit's registered scorer aligns our submitted mesh to the hidden scan (trimmed ICP from 25 starts, fixed seed per episode and slot) and converts our poses through that alignment. Mesh shape and placement therefore drive all five pose boards, not only CD-O. Pack 12e changed only mesh rows and gained +0.029 AUC, −0.75 cm RPE and one SP-SR and one MP-SR episode over 12d.
- SP-SR counts an episode as a success if the mean position error is ≤ 10 cm and the mean orientation error ≤ 0.5 rad. MP-SR needs < 3 cm and < 30° for every object. One SP-SR episode is 0.05 and could move SP-SR from 10th to 6th (−4 rank points) on 2 Nov.
- CD-O is a flat mean over 36 (episode, object) pairs; 10 of them are the spoon, which has no public scan. In late October the spoon pairs were estimated at about 0.18 cm [E].
- Across teams, AUC tracks MPPE (ρ −0.97) and RPE (−0.93) far more than CD-O (−0.51). The gap to the top is pose accuracy and failed episodes. Our RPE/MPPE ratio was 1.35 against 0.88–1.03 for the top six (31 Oct).
- Eval perception is worse than public: pack 1 lost about 19 % against our own references, against 3–4 % on public twins. On 31 Oct about 1.5 cm of board RPE was reference or registration error (rollouts vs own references 2.45 cm, board 3.93). On 3 Nov all 20 stored 14p rollouts passed SP-SR and MP-SR against our references, so the remaining 2 SP / 4 MP failures are sub-visible reference-vs-truth errors of about 2–3 cm.
- The field moved fast and late. richchentw's first scored upload (22 Oct) was already AUC 0.903, and it ended at about 0.968. DDD went from 0.73 to 0.94 within a week. SRL cut its CD-O from 0.239 to 0.210 on the last day. Our AUC rank went from 4th (25 Oct) to 8th (31 Oct) without our score getting worse.
- The oracle twin (mocap object motion as the reference) scored 0.940 against 0.595 with our perception on ep11. Near-perfect perception plus failure-free tracking explains the top scores without anything exotic.
- Upload mechanics: the Kaggle daily cap is 5 per board (reset 00:00 UTC). The written policy is 5 per week, unlimited in the final 3 days, with 2 final picks. The freeze is 4 Nov 22:00 UTC. Seven AUC uploads in 7 days went through, and dry runs on 1–2 Nov showed 18–20 uploads left per board.

### learnings_tech
- Public-scan CD-O proxies predicted the board CD-O to within about 0.0007 cm on six uploads (9a+, 12c, 12d, 13h, 13i, 13j). Pose-board predictions transferred only partly: pack 4 about 76 %, pack 2 +0.008 against +0.02–0.03, 13p2 +0.0015 against +0.004–0.006. Pack 11 over-delivered: +0.020 against +0.015.
- A registered-score gain on a single public twin can reverse on the board. The lathe pitcher gained +0.098 on its twin and cost −0.0045 on the board. Pre-registered leave-one-episode-out pose CV across public episodes did transfer (13p +0.010 AUC). Generation-source episodes must be excluded from held-out scoring (14p1 and pitcher #29 were rejected for that leakage).
- The GT-free sim_vs_ref proxy cannot rank rollouts that differ by about 0.002: pack 9b's +0.0018 proxy gain became −0.0008 on the board. From pack 10 on, a swap needs a proxy gain of at least +0.02.
- Training-box gates can fail on fresh draws on another host: eval 30 went from 0.938 to 0.768, and eval 14 plw from 0.923 to 0.826. An evaluator-gap test did not reproduce the gap (0.935 / 0.926 / 0.930), so the cause is unknown. Two-host confirmation was adopted.
- MuJoCo contact-buffer overflow (njmax) during FlashCHORD training was the hidden cause of eval 10's ~0.45 floor. Training at njmax 1024 and evaluating at stock 512/256 fixed it (+0.020 AUC). This is disclosed as a training-only setting.
- Mesh-registration flips depend on the reference preparation. The Tier B board-2 plate flips 179.18° against the raw public scan but registers at 1.05° against the budget mesh. Reverting it cost −0.0003 instead of gaining +0.02, which suggests the hidden scorer uses the budget-mode behaviour. The D1 no-flip gate was kept as a veto.
- Generated meshes (SAM 3D, TRELLIS.2, Pixal3D-MV, Hunyuan3D-2.1), chosen best-of-N against public scans (GT-informed, disclosed) and audited over 50 seeds, took CD-O from 0.26249 to 0.23856. As simulator collision shapes they broke policies whose grasps were built on the old shapes: eval 20 zero-shot 0.451 vs 0.926, eval 19 0.226 vs 0.929. Tier B collision helped only eval 14.
- Meshes chosen for scan CD-O are not the best for pose registration. The CD-O variants scored AUC 0.876–0.880 against 0.88571 on 14p, and the G3 pot had ADD 6.8 mm vs 2.7 mm. Pose-board meshes must be chosen by public pose evidence.
- Tooling: the kaggle client's token pre-check was stubbed because the proxy injects the token. The session permission guard and auto-mode classifier blocked relayed upload approvals until the owner gave a direct word. Kaggle team rename has no API call (HTTP 401). The pack-3 packing bug (cp -n into an old folder) led to empty-folder packing with a sha check and to byte-diff verification before every upload.
