## Verdict

**About 1.5–2 on a 5-point scale.** The scale runs from 1 = integration of off-the-shelf parts to 5 = new method.
The system is a careful, well-measured integration. Every stage has close prior art, often in released code for the
same Sharpa hand:
- **Perception:** FoundationStereo, SAM2/DINOv2 detection, FoundationPose. EgoX-Engine has nearly the same
  architecture, plus tracking verification.
- **Meshes:** TSDF fusion with occluder carving (Curless–Levoy, BundleSDF, TSDF++). The organisers ship
  `v2d_bundlesdf`.
- **Contact retargeting against the simulator's own collision:** mink, SPIDER, Do as I Do; EgoCPT on the Sharpa hand.
- **Flat base and start-stability check:** already in SPIDER and Do as I Do. Theirs touches the collision geometry
  only, so the scored mesh is unchanged.
- **Policy:** the organisers' FlashCHORD recipe, unchanged.

The finding that physically invalid reconstructed geometry, not perception, makes a tracking policy fail with the
assist off is CRISP's headline result (ICLR 2026, arXiv 2512.14696). We replicate it in the dexterous,
registered-ADD setting, on one episode with one seed.

## What is defensibly ours

1. **A ground-truth-free self-check, validated.** `sim_vs_ref` ranks variants like the true score: Spearman 0.98 on
   ep18 (9 policies), 0.86 on ep24, 1.0 on ep12 and ep27 [M]. `floor_vs_ref` flags harmful policies, and the
   assist-off drop test finds objects that cannot stand. The noise is measured: ±0.01–0.10 per evaluation, ≈ 0.04
   between seeds.
2. **A benchmark finding: registered scoring is very sensitive to mesh quality** [M]. In the damaged-scan test, a scan
   5 mm thicker scores 0.608 against 0.987 clean, and flat objects register flipped. Our walls are 8–15 mm thick
   against 2–7 mm in the scans. This is also a score risk, and worth telling the organisers.
3. **The physical-validity diagnosis on ep18** [M], with caveats:
   - **Confounded.** The 0.368 → 0.715 step changed both the mesh and the settling mode.
   - **Small sample.** One seed.
   - **Floor.** The do-nothing floor is 0.637, so the fix mostly removed harm: +0.08 over the floor.
4. **The only novelty candidate is narrow:** at the co-located frames (assembled or nested) of rigid objects that move
   independently through a whole video, assign the overlapping volume to one object by evidence, to recover slots and
   cavities for simulator validity. No direct precedent was found. It is not built.

## Where conventional choices cost us score

- **Hard manipulation on the validation set** (ep24 0.318, its floor 0.229): an unmodified RL recipe on a reference
  that is only kinematically cleaned, and boards without slots or standing edges.
- **The eval mix differs** [G]: 4 of 20 episodes are hard twins, 10 of 20 include the spoon, 11 of 20 have no public
  analogue. Fixing all four hard twins to 0.84 adds at most ≈ 0.07 to the mean. The 16 unvalidated episodes decide
  whether we reach the leaders.
- **Mesh thickness, a tracker with no verification, one seed per variant.**

## Compliance flags raised by the review (for the owner)

- **Room map** (`docs/research/notes/18-room-map.md`): registered with ground truth (gravity from the lean of resting
  objects in the ground truth, ground-truth table height). It must not be used at eval inference as built.
- **Hand-picked per-episode fixes on eval episodes** count as human intervention on test records: hand-chosen
  `flatten_base` frames, visual "fix the worst" passes. Every mesh or reference fix must be automatic.
- **Spoon:** `spoon/wooden_spoon.glb` is a parametric template (estimated dimensions), not a reconstruction from the
  video. The rules ask for our own reconstructed meshes. Settle this, or reconstruct it.
- **"A-lite" provenance** (owner decision, 2026-10-21): the library meshes were fused along tracks made with
  the public scans as templates. This still needs the organisers' confirmation.
- Our core models (FoundationPose, FoundationStereo) are NVIDIA non-commercial licences. This matters for the Industry
  Innovation pitch and for an Apache-2.0 code release.

## Highest value next steps (merged from the scorecard and the review; costs at ≈ $2 per 250M-step training)

1. **Floor guard:** never publish a policy whose verdict is "harmful"; fall back to an earlier checkpoint, another seed
   or a hands-away policy, chosen by `sim_vs_ref` against `floor_vs_ref`. Hours, CPU. Falsified if the guarded choice
   ever scores below the unguarded one on public episodes.
2. **Automatic support patches from every perceived rest segment** (replaces the hand patches), first as a
   SPIDER-style plate in the collision geometry only, so the scored mesh is unchanged. CPU 1–2 days, GPU A/B ≈ $8.
   Falsified if objects do not stand within 2° and 3 mm for 20 assist-off steps on public episodes 12/18/23/24/27, or
   ep18 with the automatic version scores below 0.65 (2 seeds × 6 evaluations).
3. **Thinner meshes:** mesh the TSDF zero crossing, then run the organisers' `v2d_bundlesdf` as a baseline before
   writing a new fine TSDF. CPU 1–2 days + a perception box ≈ $3. Falsified if registered AUC on ep2/11/21/39/41 does
   not beat library_v2 by > 0.01.
4. **Warm-start eval policies from their public twins** (25↔24, 19/30↔18, 20↔21). Public test ep23 → ep24 at equal
   steps, 2 seeds × 6 evaluations ≈ $8. Falsified if the gain is ≤ 0.03.
5. **Reward and training budget on ep24:** port FlashDexRetarget's object-centric reward, try 500M steps and a slower
   assist decay. ≈ $12. Falsified if ep24 stays ≤ 0.33.
6. **Physics search on the hard windows** (SPIDER / Do as I Do sampler on the evaluator's own MuJoCo scene, starting
   with ep24 f95–135). 2–4 days, the largest and most uncertain gain. Falsified if no sample keeps board 2 within
   2 cm / 15° for ≥ 30 frames open loop.
7. **Interaction carving at co-located frames** (the narrow novelty item; ep24 / eval 25, nested cups, lid on pot).
   CPU 1–2 days. Falsified if the assembled-pose overlap does not fall to the scan floor (the reviewer measured the
   scans overlapping ≈ 4 cm³ at our tracks, i.e. tracking error; not rechecked by me).
8. **A generative prior only in never-observed voxels** (TRELLIS.2 is MIT-licensed; the organisers' baseline uses
   SAM 3D). 2–3 days. Falsified if registered AUC on ep39/21/11/31 does not beat library_v2 by > 0.01.
