## 3. What the numbers show [D], and what they do not

**1. Swapping an anchor object moves the other objects' frames.**

The kit's `_align_registered_scene` sets one world rotation from the first object without a symmetry axis at frame 0,
and sets the offset from object 0. So a swap of the anchor object changes the other objects' scores even when its
own score stays put.
- **Pot on ep2:** the pot is the anchor and the lid has an order-2 axis. The pot swap left the pot's own AUC flat
  (0.9923 -> 0.9931) but dropped the lid's AUC from 0.9723 to 0.9397. Episode dAUC -0.0159, the worst pot twin.
- **ep2, both swapped (c0):** the episode loss shrinks to -0.0040.
- **ep13, objects [blue_cup, plastic_dish_rack]:** the rack is the anchor and the blue cup sets the offset.
  - Single swaps: rack +0.0056, cup -0.0049.
  - Joint: -0.0240. Both per-object AUCs fall (rack 0.9882 -> 0.9724, cup 0.9329 -> 0.9007).
  - So the joint delta is not the sum of the single deltas. The rule tests single swaps. The board will see the
    joint swap.
- **ep12:** this goes the other way. Rack -0.0092 alone, joint +0.0240.

**2. Lid: one informative twin.** On ep0 the lid's AUC is 0.0 in every run, which is the known failed lid track
(PREREG caveat). So the lid verdict rests on ep2 alone (+0.0111). n = 2 is what the rule fixed.

**3. Blue cup on ep21: a raw registration rotation of 146.9 deg** (base 1.36 deg).
- What the scores show:
  - ep21 holds [blue_cup, water_pitcher], so the pitcher sets the scene rotation and the cup's own registration
    counts.
  - The cup's symmetric per-object AUC still rose (0.9815 -> 0.9852), so the cup axis is not tipped over.
  - [H] The rotation is mostly a spin about the cup axis, which every kit metric treats as symmetric.
  - The stage-1 record also shows 0/7 symmetry-aware flips for this mesh.
- The same twin has the largest dRPE of any run (+0.747 cm) and the only SP/MP-SR change (MP-SR 0 -> 1).
- [H] An off-axis anchor combined with a spun registration puts the anchor point a few mm off, which RPE picks up.
- Diagnostic that would falsify this: split R into spin about the scan axis and tilt of that axis, for ep21
  slot 0 with seed 1_000_003*21.
  - It reads the scan, so under PREREG §8 it was **not** run here. It needs its own go-ahead.

**4. SP-SR is unchanged on every twin.** MP-SR changes only on ep21. These two boards will barely react on the twins.

**5. Not tested here.**
- The board scores the simulator rollouts, not perception tracks. It uses the hidden scans, the host's anchor and
  point sampling, and different eval episodes and seeds.
- [H] The board's AUC change can differ in sign from the twins'. The 26 Oct pitcher swap is the precedent.
- The joint swap is the configuration the board will see. On the twins its mean is positive (+0.0148), but it has
  one twin at -0.024.
