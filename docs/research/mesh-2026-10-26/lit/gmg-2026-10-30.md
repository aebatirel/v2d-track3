# Generalized mesh generator (GMG): focused literature and open-source review

Date: 2026-10-30. Written for the GMG design stage. CPU only, $0, **zero new scan scorings**. No other repo file was
changed. This note extends `generative.md`, `video-physics.md` and `primitives-cad.md` (26 Oct) and does not repeat
them; where an older fact matters it is cited again with its primary link.

Organiser code was read at `nvidia-isaac/video_to_data` commit `7eb9721` (2026-10-20), sparse clone in the scratchpad.
Links below point at that commit (`https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721.../reconstruction/modules/...`).

**Labels**
- **[D]** measured by us against a public scan (diagnostic only).
- **[M]** measured without our scans: either reported by the cited source under its own protocol (never CD-O), or
  measured by us without a scan.
- **[R]** read directly in primary code, official docs or a vendor catalogue. A fact about software or products, not a
  measurement. (Added to the brief's label set so that code facts are not passed off as measurements.)
- **[E]** estimate. **[H]** hypothesis; each names the experiment that would falsify it.
- **[U]** unverified: secondary source, search-result snippet, or an author claim with no number behind it.

Our setting, for every fit judgement: FoundationStereo depth at 0.5 scale (focal 600.2 px, baseline 75.5 mm, work log
line 107 [R]); our object tracks `cam_T_<obj>`; 10 small tabletop objects; partial, hand-occluded views; 4096-face budget;
CD-O is similarity-invariant but ADD/AUC are not; CPU-only local box (4 cores, 15 GB); Vast GPUs at ~$0.3–1.0/h.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The organisers' SAM 3D wrapper already takes metric depth.** `v2d_sam3d` builds a pointmap from a depth map plus
   intrinsics and an optional mask, passes it to SAM 3D, and saves the predicted layout (rotation, translation, scale) in
   the OpenCV camera frame to `transform.json` [R] ([image_to_mesh.py L146–169, L190–266](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/image_to_mesh.py#L146-L266)).
   The layout is de-normalised with the pointmap's own scale and shift, so a metric pointmap gives a metric layout [R]
   ([inference_pipeline_modified.py L435–443](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/inference_pipeline_modified.py#L435-L443)). Our
   generator probe never used this: it ran SAM 3D's own MoGe on a crop and did not save the layout [R]
   (`gen/gen_sam3d.py` line 4; `diag-2026-10-30/README.md`). With our stereo pointmap on the full rectified frame and our
   `cam_T_obj`, each SAM 3D mesh lands in our object frame with no registration step and no library_v3 [H]. Falsifier
   below (§3.1).
2. **The organisers' SRT fit is a silhouette fit with an optional depth term that ignores visibility.** It minimises a
   clipped Huber distance-transform term, a mask-coverage term and a rasterised IoU term over ≤25 views with Powell, and
   then fits a per-axis (anisotropic) scale [R] ([scale_mesh_srt.py L579–659, L1012+](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/lib/scale_mesh_srt.py#L579-L659)).
   Its depth term compares *every* projected vertex, front and back, with the observed front depth [R]; that biases the
   fit like our own `sim3_fit.py` did [H]. It is pure numpy/scipy/OpenCV, so it can run on our CPU box [R].
3. **The organisers' BundleSDF module accepts known poses.** `v2d_bundlesdf` trains the SDF from given
   `cam_in_ob` poses, depth and masks; their 19-keyframe example took 123 s SDF + 58 s texture on an RTX A6000 [M]
   ([README](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/README.md), [run_time.yaml](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/assets/stage1_example/run_time.yaml)).
   Its truncation is ≥1 cm and its octree voxel 2 cm by default [R] ([config.yaml L218–L284](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/assets/stage1_example/config.yaml#L218-L284)),
   coarser than our 2–4 mm walls, and BundleSDF itself is research-only licensed [R] ([LICENSE.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVlabs/BundleSDF/master/LICENSE.txt)).
4. **`v2d_gsplat_refinement` refines poses, not shape.** It optimises per-frame object and hand poses, mesh-anchored
   Gaussian appearance and one global object scale, and writes rigid poses plus a scale; it does not export a refined
   mesh [R] ([refine.py L1–25, L455–470](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_gsplat_refinement/lib/refine.py#L1-L25)). Useful for ADD, not for CD-O.
5. **Every published generate-then-fit pipeline we found stops at rigid / similarity / per-axis scale.** AGILE optimises
   pose plus anisotropic scale [M] ([AGILE](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.04672)); SRT the same [R]; MV-SAM3D a similarity
   with fixed geometry [M] ([MV-SAM3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11633)); CARI4D scale only
   ([CARI4D](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11988v1)). Nobody reports a measured gain from non-rigid refinement of a
   generated mesh against real depth. Non-rigid fitting stays a hypothesis [H].
6. **Multi-view SAM 3D works on real hand-held video.** GraG builds its canonical object with MV-SAM3D from keyframes
   and reports HO3Dv3 CD 0.51 cm, F10 97.2 %, against HOLD 0.78 cm and MagicHOI 1.58 cm, at 0.27 h per 100 frames on one
   RTX 4090 [M] ([GraG](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.12929v2)). GraG code was not found [U]. MV-SAM3D code is public under
   the SAM Licence and expects Depth-Anything-3 poses and pointmaps, which our tracks and stereo depth could replace [R/H]
   ([repo](https://github.com/devinli123/MV-SAM3D)).
7. **No paper validates a GT-free estimator of full-shape accuracy from partial RGB-D views.** The evidence is indirect:
   SAM 3D keeps a layout refinement only if mask IoU rises, and that raised ADT 3D IoU 0.484 → 0.526 [M]
   ([SAM 3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16624v2)); Bayes' Rays uncertainty tracks NeRF depth error (AUSE) [M]
   ([CVPR 2024](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Goli_Bayes_Rays_Uncertainty_Quantification_for_Neural_Radiance_Fields_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf));
   MEt3R and COLMAP-style consistency metrics are validated against human judgement, not geometry [M/U]. Our own
   measurement is the best evidence for our data: Spearman 0.46 between the GT-free metric and CD-O over 104 meshes [D]
   (`results/gen-probe/README.md`). New ranking ideas can be tested against **existing** scores (§4.4).
8. **Symmetry: the classic scorer is asymmetric and free-space based.** Thrun & Wegbreit score each mirrored point as
   matched (+1), occluded (0) or unexplained (−15 in their example) [R] ([US7961934](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7961934);
   [ICCV 2005](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2005.221)). That is the same structure as our `dr_fsv` gate, applied to
   symmetry hypotheses.
9. **Marker-ball radius can come from vendor catalogues, not scans.** Commercial passive markers span about 2.5–25 mm
   diameter (§5.2) [R/U]. With f = 600.2 px at z ≈ 0.5 m, a 14 mm ball is ≈ 17 px across and a 9.5 mm ball ≈ 11 px [E].
   Fit the radius from our points (geometric fit after a Taubin/Hyper start, because algebraic fits shrink small caps
   [M] ([Al-Sharadqah & Chernov 2009](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.0421))), or choose among catalogue sizes by likelihood.
10. **Provenance residue.** The eval tracks were made with `MESH_DIR=meshes/library_v3` [R] (`ops/jobs/eval_depth.sh`
    line 11), like the public tracks. Any mesh placed "in our tracked frame" therefore inherits library_v3's frame
    convention. §6 lists GT-free ways out and what each costs.

## 1. Organiser `video_to_data` modules (code read, commit 7eb9721)

### 1.1 `v2d_sam3d`: does it accept an external metric pointmap? Yes.
- **Inputs.** `image_to_mesh(...)` takes `depth_path`, `depth_intrinsics_path`, `depth_mask_path`, or a MoGe
  `pointmap_path` [R] ([L190–203](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/image_to_mesh.py#L190-L203)); the CLI exposes the same flags
  (`--depth_path`, `--depth_intrinsics_path`, `--depth_mask_path`) [R] ([L284–286](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/image_to_mesh.py#L284-L286)).
- **Pointmap build.** `_depth_to_pointmap` back-projects depth with fx, fy, cx, cy in OpenCV convention, sets
  non-finite, non-positive and **out-of-mask** pixels to NaN "so SAM3D's pose decoder only sees object-region depth",
  then rotates into PyTorch3D camera space [R] ([L146–169](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/image_to_mesh.py#L146-L169)).
- **Model path.** `InferencePipelinePointMap.compute_pointmap(image, pointmap)` uses the supplied pointmap instead of
  MoGe and resizes it (nearest) to the image size; intrinsics are then re-inferred from the pointmap [R]
  ([L270–325](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/inference_pipeline_modified.py#L270-L325)). The wrapper overrides the saved intrinsics with
  the caller's, because the re-inferred ones "can be inaccurate" [R] ([L250–264](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/image_to_mesh.py#L250-L264)).
- **Metric layout.** The pose decoder receives `scene_scale=pointmap_scale, scene_shift=pointmap_shift` [R]
  ([L435–443](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/inference_pipeline_modified.py#L435-L443)). So the output (R, t, s) is in the units of the
  pointmap. The wrapper converts it to an OpenCV object-to-camera transform and saves `transform.json` [R]
  ([`_to_opencv_transform` L114–143](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_sam3d/lib/image_to_mesh.py#L114-L143)).
- **Optional layout refinement** (`with_layout_postprocess`, off by default): ICP of the mesh to the pointmap points
  (threshold 0.05), kept only if mask IoU rises, then render-and-compare, kept only if IoU ≥ 0.5 and rises [R]
  ([sam3d_objects inference_utils.py L83–235](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-objects/blob/main/sam3d_objects/pipeline/inference_utils.py#L83-L235)).
- **Upstream** `notebook/inference.py` also exposes `pointmap=` [R] ([inference.py](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-objects/blob/main/notebook/inference.py)).
  Needs ≥32 GB VRAM [R] ([setup.md](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-objects/blob/main/doc/setup.md)).
- **Paper.** The pointmap may come from sensors or monocular depth; it "minimally affects" shape (48 % vs 48 % preference
  on LVIS) [M] ([SAM 3D App. E.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16624v2)). The paper gives no layout ablation with sensor
  depth [U: not found]. Layout on ADT: 3D IoU 0.497, ICP-rotation 15.3°, ADD-S 0.077 of diameter [M] (same, Table 3).
- **The HOI pipeline does not pass depth to SAM 3D.** It calls `run_image_to_mesh(image, mask, ..., seed)` only [R]
  ([run_reconstruction.py L1075–1083](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/docker/run_reconstruction.py#L1075-L1083));
  `--sam3d_use_depth` only switches on the SRT depth term [R] ([L284, L692, L1091–1094](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/docker/run_reconstruction.py#L1091-L1094)).
- **Fit for us.**
  - The pointmap must be pixel-aligned with the RGB given to SAM 3D. Feed the rectified frame that the stereo depth
    belongs to, at full frame (not a crop), with that camera's K [R: the wrapper pairs depth with its own intrinsics].
  - Mask = our object mask minus hand pixels. Out-of-mask depth is NaN by design [R].
  - Then `obj_T_mesh = inv(cam_T_obj) · cam_T_mesh` puts each per-frame mesh in our object frame [H].
  - Cost: median 11.3 s per run on H100 NVL in our probe (shape only, crop input) [M] (`gen_logs/sam3d.jsonl`);
    841 s for 13 frames on an RTX A6000 in the organiser reference run [M]
    ([README L621–629](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/README.md#reference-performance)).

### 1.2 SRT (scale-rotation-translation) fit in `v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction`
- **Objective per view** [R] ([evaluate_srt L579–659](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/lib/scale_mesh_srt.py#L579-L659)):
  - Huber(δ = 3 px) of the distance transform to the mask at each projected vertex, clipped at max(20 px, 0.1 × mask
    extent): vertices outside the mask cost;
  - coverage weight 0.5: mask-boundary pixels to the nearest projected vertex;
  - IoU weight 2.0: 1 − IoU of a dilated vertex splat against the mask at ¼ resolution
    ([`_raster_iou` L141–171](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/lib/scale_mesh_srt.py#L141-L171));
  - depth weight 1.0 (if `use_depth`): |z_vertex − z_obs| · f / z_obs at the vertex pixel, Huber, clipped [R]
    ([L644–655](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/lib/scale_mesh_srt.py#L644-L655)).
- **Search.** With a SAM 3D orientation prior (from `transform.json` and the source-frame pose): uniform STR, then
  anisotropic per-axis scale in mesh-local axes. Without a prior: 48 signed axis permutations, top-k fully optimised
  [R] ([L564–576, L1198+](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/lib/scale_mesh_srt.py#L564-L576)). Powell, 60 iterations
  by default, ≤25 views [R] ([README Key Options](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/README.md#key-options-1)).
- **Ranking of candidates.** Lowest SRT loss plus small penalties for odd scale, anisotropy > 1.5, small mesh,
  stage-2 source frame [R] ([select_sam3d_best.py L198–255](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/lib/select_sam3d_best.py#L198-L255)).
  The README calls it a suggestion and says a complete-looking mesh "is not by itself evidence that unseen geometry,
  proportions, or metric scale are accurate" [R] ([README](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/README.md#reference-performance)).
- **Runtime.** SRT was the largest stage, ≈37 min for 13 candidates with 8 parallel workers on the 2× A6000 host [M].
- **Fit for us.**
  - Our object moves, but `cam_T_obj` gives object-frame cameras, which is exactly the "stationary object, moving
    camera" contract of `--sam3d_capture_mode stationary` [R/H].
  - **Visibility bug [H].** The depth term is not z-buffered: back-facing and occluded vertices are compared with the
    front depth. Back vertices > ~1.7 cm behind the front (20 px × 0.5 m / 600 px) hit the clip and give no gradient;
    vertices near the front are pulled to it. Expected effect: a pull toward the camera and thinning along view rays,
    like our biased `sim3_fit.py`. Falsifier: render depth and masks of a known mesh along one of our public tracks,
    perturb it by a known Sim(3), run SRT with depth; the hypothesis fails if the recovered translation error along the
    mean view ray is < 2 mm on 5/5 objects. Zero scan scorings.
  - **Hand occlusion [H].** The distance-transform term penalises mesh outside the object mask, so hand-occluded pixels
    shrink the mesh. Treat object ∪ hand as "allowed" for that term.
  - **The anisotropic step is the right tool for SAM 3D's single-view stretch** (lid diameter ×0.7, thickness ×1.6 in
    our diagnosis [D], `diag-2026-10-30/README.md`) [H]. Falsifier: after anisotropic SRT over ≥10 views, the lid's
    principal extents still differ by > 10 % from the extents of our own observed points (GT-free).
  - CPU: pure numpy/scipy/cv2 [R]; on 4 cores, ~1–2 h per object-episode for 6–13 candidates [E] from the 37 min on
    8 workers.

### 1.3 `v2d_bundlesdf` (known-pose SDF training)
- **Inputs.** RGB, depth, masks, `keyframes.yml` with a `cam_in_ob` 4×4 per keyframe, calibration with baseline [R]
  ([README](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/README.md); [keyframes.yml](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/assets/stage1_example/keyframes.yml)).
  This matches our tracks directly (`cam_in_ob = inv(cam_T_obj)`) [R/H].
- **Defaults.** `trunc 0.01` m, auto-tune trunc = 0.07 × diameter clamped to [1, 5] cm, octree smallest voxel 2 cm,
  hash grid finest 256, 3000 steps [R] ([config.yaml L180–L290](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/assets/stage1_example/config.yaml#L180-L290)).
- **Runtime.** 19 keyframes: 123 s SDF, 58 s texture, RTX A6000 [M] ([run_time.yaml](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/assets/stage1_example/run_time.yaml)).
  Full HOI BundleSDF workflow (2,433 stereo pairs, with SfM and tracking): ≈100 min on 2× A6000 [M]. Needs ≥24 GB;
  Blackwell (sm_120) unsupported [R] ([README GPU](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/README.md#gpu-compatibility)).
- **Accuracy.** HO3D CD 0.57 cm, but only over faces seen in the video [M]
  ([BundleSDF App. C](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2303.14158)). On occluded objects BundleSDF scores 2.98 cm [M]
  ([Vysics](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.18719v1)). Organiser README: thin or partly seen parts "can be missing or
  thickened" [R].
- **Licence.** Wrapper Apache-2.0 [R] ([LICENSE](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/LICENSE)); the Docker build pulls NVlabs/BundleSDF
  [R] ([Dockerfile L248–253](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_bundlesdf/docker/Dockerfile#L248-L253)), whose licence is "research or evaluation
  purposes only" [R] ([LICENSE.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVlabs/BundleSDF/master/LICENSE.txt)). Winners
  must release code under Apache-2.0 [R] ([FAQ](https://nvidia-isaac.github.io/video_to_data/v2d_challenge/assets/v2d_challenge_2026_faqs.pdf)).
  Calling it as an external dependency is probably acceptable since the organisers do the same [H; ask organisers].
- **Fit for us.** A cheap second "observed-surface" candidate next to our TSDF, using our tracks. The ≥1 cm
  truncation will thicken our 2–4 mm walls [H]; `auto_tune.mesh_resolution` (2–8 mm) is off by default [R]. It does
  not complete undersides. Falsifier for "worth running": on 5 public pairs, its GT-free one-sided depth residual is
  not below our TSDF's.

### 1.4 `v2d_gsplat_refinement`
- **What it optimises.** `refine.py`: object Gaussian attributes anchored to the mesh (offsets Δp regularised,
  outward normal weight 100), per-frame object pose, MANO hand pose; losses: masked photometric L1, silhouettes,
  optional MoGe depth Huber, temporal smoothness [R] ([refine.py L1–25, L225–310](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_gsplat_refinement/lib/refine.py#L1-L25)).
  `refine_simple.py` does not optimise Gaussian offsets at all [R] ([L1–20](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_gsplat_refinement/lib/refine_simple.py#L1-L20)).
- **Outputs.** Rigid object poses with unit scale, an optional global scale JSON, refined hand poses, overlay video [R]
  ([L455–470](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_gsplat_refinement/lib/refine.py#L455-L470)). No refined mesh.
- **Licence / deps.** Apache-2.0; gsplat 1.4.0 (Apache-2.0) [R] ([Dockerfile](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_gsplat_refinement/docker/Dockerfile);
  [gsplat LICENSE](https://github.com/nerfstudio-project/gsplat/blob/main/LICENSE)).
- **Fit for us.** Not a shape tool. A possible later pose refiner once the mesh is fixed. It uses MoGe depth, not
  stereo; swapping in our metric depth needs code changes [R/E].

### 1.5 `mesh_to_usd` (simulator copy)
- Builds a metre-scale, Z-up rigid USD with explicit **CoACD** colliders, mass (default 0.3 kg, marked as assumed),
  centre of mass and collider-derived inertia, then an Isaac Sim drop test whose initial pose can be the *recorded
  support pose* from the video [R] ([mesh_to_usd README](https://github.com/nvidia-isaac/video_to_data/blob/7eb9721088615fe9201634a6be5cf48b049d1090/reconstruction/modules/v2d_hoi_object_reconstruction/mesh_to_usd/README.md)).
  CoACD is MIT [R] ([LICENSE](https://github.com/SarahWeiii/CoACD/blob/main/LICENSE)).
- **Fit for us.** A GT-free "rests correctly" check exists in principle: compare the mesh's settled pose with the
  tracked at-rest pose. `trimesh.poses.compute_stable_poses` is a CPU stand-in [R] ([docs](https://trimesh.org/trimesh.poses.html)).
  Watertightness must be checked on the kit-decoded 4096-face mesh, as gates v3 already does [R] (`autokit/gates.py`).

### 1.6 `evaluate_mesh_quality.py`
- Rigid registration, RMS-surface-radius scale normalisation, unsquared symmetric Chamfer as % of the reference

## 3. Generative priors conditioned on our geometry (question 2)

### 3.1 SAM 3D Objects with our metric pointmap (single view, many frames)
- **Code path:** §1.1. Accepts depth or pointmap; returns mesh + metric layout in the camera frame [R].
- **Hypothesis H-SAM-PM [H].** With our stereo pointmap on full rectified frames and hand-free masks, the per-frame
  layout mapped through `inv(cam_T_obj)` places the mesh within 10 mm (centroid) and 15° of our observed points, without
  any ICP. **Falsifier (GT-free):** on ≥6 public objects × ≥4 frames, median centroid offset to the visible-surface
  points > 10 mm, or median pairwise rotation spread between frames > 15° (excluding the declared symmetries).
  Our probe's crop-plus-MoGe route gave 15–33 mm translation errors after our own fit [D], so this is a real test.
- **Hypothesis H-SAM-CONS [H].** Per-frame meshes that agree with each other (low pairwise Chamfer in our object
  frame) are more accurate. Falsifier in §4.4.
- **Compute [E].** 33 public pairs × 6 frames × 1 seed ≈ 200 runs × 11–65 s ≈ 0.6–3.6 GPU-h, plus ~1 h setup (SAM 3D
  env was built once before; 12.4 GB weights already in the HF store [R] `results/gen-probe/README.md`) → ≈ $1–5.
  Eval: about the same number of pairs (20 episodes vs 20 public episodes with 33 pairs) [E] → ≈ $1–5.

### 3.2 Multi-view conditioning
- **MV-SAM3D** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11633); [code](https://github.com/devinli123/MV-SAM3D), SAM Licence [R]).
  - Training-free. At each flow step it queries SAM 3D once per view and averages the velocities with weights from
    attention entropy and voxel visibility (α = β = 30, γ = 0.5); then a similarity-only pose refinement with Chamfer to
    depth points, 100 Adam steps [M].
  - Poses and metric pointmaps come from Depth Anything 3 [M]. **We would substitute our tracks and stereo pointmaps**
    [H]; the README's `--da3_output npz` is the interface to replace [R].
  - GSO-30 per-object CD (×10⁻³): 20.2 with 2 views, 17.3 with 5, vs single-view SAM 3D 42.0 [M]. Not like-for-like
    (no multi-view SAM 3D baseline). Ran on one A100-80GB; no runtime given [M].
  - Limitation stated: entropy weighting is fooled by symmetric or repetitive structure [M]. Our cups, boards and
    sweeper are exactly that [H].
  - **Fit.** Best-matched published method to "SAM 3D + our poses + our depth". Cost ≈ N views × single-view time
    [E]. Falsifier: on the probe's 10 objects, 6-view MV-SAM3D CD-O mean is not below single-view SAM 3D's 0.501 [D]
    (needs pre-registered scan scorings, so it belongs to a later stage).
- **Pixal3D-MV** (ours, measured): mean CD-O 0.490, median centroid offset 5.4 mm, 6/20 flips (boards, sweeper) [D]
  (`results/gen-probe/README.md`, `diag-2026-10-30/README.md`). It generates in the frame of the given cameras [R]
  ([repo](https://github.com/TencentARC/Pixal3D)). Our view sets were mostly *one* view; with 3 views the dust pan went
  0.628 → 0.240 [D]. The bottleneck was view selection (hand rule), not the model [H].
- **ReconViaGen, Hunyuan3D-2mv, TRELLIS multi-image** were reviewed on 26 Oct (`lit/generative.md` §3): unposed or
  fixed-name views, canonical output frame; ReconViaGen 0.059 vs Hunyuan3D-2mv 0.124 on OmniObject3D 4-view [M]
  ([ReconViaGen](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23306v1)). They need our registration step, which is the step that failed
  [D]. Lower priority than MV-SAM3D and Pixal3D-MV, which use our cameras.

### 3.3 Point-cloud-conditioned completion
- **Hunyuan3D-Omni** (point, voxel, bbox, skeleton control; 10 GB VRAM) [R] ([README](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Omni)).
  Licence excludes the EU, UK and South Korea, including outputs [R] (26 Oct review,
  [License.txt](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Omni/blob/main/License.txt)). RecGen3D (VGGT points →

- **Points-to-3D** (CVPR 2026): point cloud replaces TRELLIS's sparse-structure noise init, plus a trained structure
  inpainting network; preserves visible regions [M: claim, no numbers on the abstract page] ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18782)).
  No code found [U].
- **InpaintSLat** (May 2026): training-free TRELLIS latent inpainting by initial-noise optimisation [U: abstract only]
  ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00664)). No code found.
- **GenPC**: keep observed points, generate only the missing part, scale sweep 0.8–1.2 + ICP; Redwood CD×100 1.98 →

- **Fit.** "Keep observed, generate missing" is the right shape for GMG [H]. The only released, licence-clean tools
  for it are GenPC-style post-hoc merging and SAM 3D's own pointmap conditioning. Points-to-3D/InpaintSLat are ideas

## 4. GT-free mesh quality estimation and candidate ranking (question 3)

### 4.1 What the literature offers
- **Render-and-compare acceptance.** SAM 3D accepts a refined layout only if mask IoU rises; on average that raised 3D
  IoU [M] (§3.4). SRT ranks candidates by its own loss [R] (§1.2). Neither reports how often the ranking picks the best
  shape.
- **Free-space / visibility scoring.** Thrun & Wegbreit's symmetry scorer: each predicted point is matched, occluded
  (no penalty) or unexplained (strong penalty, e.g. −15 vs +1) [R] ([patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7961934)).
  Co-Section's hull term (observed free space) improved completeness 42–71 % but made accuracy worse on 2/3 objects [M]
  ([ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2004.04630)).
- **Uncertainty as an error proxy.** Bayes' Rays: post-hoc NeRF uncertainty correlates with depth error, measured by
  AUSE, better than CF-NeRF [M] ([paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Goli_Bayes_Rays_Uncertainty_Quantification_for_Neural_Radiance_Fields_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf)).
  This is per-pixel depth error on observed views, not unseen geometry.
- **Consistency metrics validated against humans, not geometry.** MEt3R (DUSt3R warping, no poses needed) [R]
  ([CVPR 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Asim_MET3R_Measuring_Multi-View_Consistency_in_Generated_Images_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf));
  COLMAP-based multi-view consistency reported up to 4× higher correlation with human judgement than MEt3R [U]
  ([alphaXiv 2605.18754](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2605.18754)).
- **Appearance does not guarantee geometry.** RaDe-GS and SolidGS show rendering quality and Chamfer can move
  apart [U: search summary] ([RaDe-GS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.01467), [SolidGS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.15400)).
  So photometric held-out error is a weak shape ranker [H].
- **VLM critics.** AGILE rejects synthesised views with a VLM critic [M] ([AGILE](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.04672)); no
  measure of ranking accuracy against geometry.
- **Gap.** I found no paper that measures rank correlation between a GT-free score and full-shape Chamfer on partly
  observed real objects [U: absence after 6 searches].

### 4.2 What we have measured
- Gen-probe GT-free metric (median obs→mesh + mesh→obs): Spearman 0.46 over 104 meshes; per object median 0.58
  (−0.21 to 0.72); argmin picks the CD-O best on 2/10 objects [D] (`results/gen-probe/README.md`).
- Gates v3 reject 6/7 good program fits (specificity problem) [D] (brief; `results/autokit-stage1/`).
- Pixal3D held-out views: reprojection IoU 0.44–0.67 with one view vs 0.88–0.97 with all views, and the dust-pan CD-O
  fell 0.628 → 0.240 [D/M]. Held-out silhouettes do see large shape errors.

### 4.3 Why observation-only scores are weak here [H]
- They are blind to unseen surfaces (undersides, interiors) and to wall thickness behind a seen face. That is where
  our CD-O loss sits (`lit/video-physics.md` §5).
- One-sided distances reward meshes that hug the visible side; the biased `sim3_fit.py` objective is the same effect
  (17/20 wrong placements scored better than scan-ideal) [D] (`diag-2026-10-30/README.md`).
- Generated meshes come with different priors per model, so a score that only sees observed surface ranks models by
  how well they hug observations, not by how well they guess the hidden part.

### 4.4 Ranking hypotheses that can be tested with zero new scan scorings
All use the 104 frozen gen-probe meshes and their existing CD-O scores (`results/gen-probe/rows.json`,
`scores.jsonl`), plus existing autokit rows. Pre-register the metric definitions and the decision rule *before*
computing any correlation; report the result as [D]; do not tune weights on it (that would import scan information into
a constant).
- **R1 visibility-aware two-sided residual [H].** Z-buffered depth residual on the mesh's *first* surface, plus
  free-space violation (dr_fsv), with hand pixels masked. Falsified if Spearman with CD-O ≤ 0.46 (the current metric).
- **R2 held-out temporal blocks [H].** Fit on 4 of 5 time blocks; score silhouette IoU and z-buffered depth on the held
  block. Falsified if Spearman ≤ R1's, or if argmin hits the CD-O best on ≤ 2/10 objects.
- **R3 cross-candidate consensus [H].** Score each mesh by its median Chamfer (after its own GT-free placement) to the
  other candidates of the same object (other seeds, models, frames). A medoid is a classic robust pick; MV-SAM3D's
  entropy weighting is the same idea inside the model [M]. Falsified if the medoid's CD-O is not below the per-object
  median candidate on ≥ 7/10 objects.
- **R4 asymmetric costs (Thrun–Wegbreit) [H].** Unexplained (in seen free space) ≫ occluded (no cost). Falsified if
  adding the asymmetry does not raise Spearman over R1 by ≥ 0.05.
- Expected honest outcome [E]: some gain on large errors (dust pan, flips) and little on 1–2 mm wall or base errors;
  plan the pipeline so that wall/base choices do not depend on the ranker.

## 5. Symmetry and marker balls (question 4)

### 5.1 Symmetry detection and symmetry-based completion
- **Detection.**
  - Line-geometry axis fit for surfaces of revolution, already reviewed (`lit/primitives-cad.md` §2.1;
    [Pottmann & Randrup](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/rotational-and-helical-surface-approximation-for-reverse-engineer/)) [R].
  - Covariance-eigenvector axis for rotationally symmetric clouds (needs a complete cloud) [U]
    ([Applied Math. Letters 2025](https://dspace.zcu.cz/items/77d3caba-10a7-48de-8167-bcc2c554bcdb)).
  - **SymSeg** (Ecins, Fermüller, Aloimonos, IROS 2018): rotational and reflectional symmetry from multi-view point
    clouds *plus an occupancy map* used to reason about occlusion; C++, PCL + OctoMap; includes a single-object
    reflectional detector that needs consistently oriented normals [R] ([repo README](https://github.com/aecins/symseg)).
    No licence file found [U]. A later learned method claims higher precision/recall than Ecins under heavy occlusion on
    YCB-Video [M: PR curves only] ([arXiv 2003.06520](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.06520)).
  - SymmetryNet and NeRD (learned) were reviewed on 26 Oct (`lit/primitives-cad.md` §3).
- **Completion.**
  - Thrun & Wegbreit 2005: detect probable symmetries in partial active-stereo range data, extend into occluded space,
    per-part symmetries; scoring as in §4.1 [R] ([patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7961934)).
  - Sung et al. 2015: part and symmetry structure estimated from a single Kinect scan, fused with database priors to
    fill occluded regions; evaluated on 481 synthetic shapes and Kinect scans [U: listing] ([Adobe](https://research.adobe.com/publication/data-driven-structural-priors-for-shape-completion);
    [code](https://github.com/mhsung/structure-completion)).
  - SymmCompletion: ≈4 % CD gain on PCN [M] (26 Oct review, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18007v1)).
- **Fit for us [H].**
  - Generic rule, no per-object code: propose symmetry hypotheses (continuous rotation, C2, mirror) from our observed
    points; accept one only if Thrun–Wegbreit-style scoring against our vote grid passes (mirrored points must not land
    in seen free space); then complete only into unseen voxels.
  - Never let a symmetry overwrite observed asymmetric features (handles, spout, balls, slots). Those decide
    registration flips (sweeper: 3/30 flips without balls, 0/30 with them [D]).
  - Falsifier (GT-free, zero scorings): on the public pairs, the accepted symmetry for an object differs between two
    episodes of the same object (e.g. "rotational" in one, "none" in the other) in > 20 % of objects.

### 5.2 Small spheres (mocap markers) in noisy point clouds
- **Domain prior from vendor catalogues, not scans.**
  - Vicon: 3, 4, 6.4, 9.5, 14, 19, 25.4 mm diameters [U: search snippet of Vicon help page; the page did not load]
    ([Vicon help](https://vicon-help.atlassian.net/wiki/x/k9Aa)); a reseller lists hard markers 3–38 mm [U]
    ([prophysics](https://www.prophysics.ch/en/?p=11275)).
  - OptiTrack: 6.4, 7.9, 9.5, 11, 12.7, 14, 15.9, 19 mm [U: search snippet; page returned HTTP 429]
    ([accessories](https://optitrack.com/accessories)).
  - Qualisys: facial 2.5 and 4 mm; threaded underwater 12–50 mm [R] ([markers](https://www.qualisys.com/accessories/markers/)).
  - So a generic prior is "diameter 2.5–25 mm, usually from a small discrete set" [E]. Small objects and props use the
    smaller sizes [U: Vicon help snippet].
- **Detection and fitting.**
  - RANSAC with radius limits: PCL `SACSegmentation::setRadiusLimits` with `SACMODEL_SPHERE` [R]
    ([PCL docs](https://pointclouds.org/documentation/classpcl_1_1_s_a_c_segmentation.html)); pyransac3d `Sphere`,
    Apache-2.0 [R] ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pyransac3d/), [LICENSE](https://github.com/leomariga/pyRANSAC-3D/blob/master/LICENSE)).
  - Known-radius RANSAC with distance-only acceptance, then z-score outlier removal, beat plain RANSAC on scanner
    targets [U: abstract] ([Mathematics 2023](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/11/4/1023)). Many methods need the radius in
    advance and a large visible part of the sphere [U] ([PMC 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9371188)).
  - **Fit bias on small caps.** The Kåsa algebraic fit is biased toward smaller radii on incomplete arcs, and the bias
    grows without bound as the arc shrinks; Taubin and Hyper fits remove the essential bias [M]
    ([Al-Sharadqah & Chernov 2009](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.0421); [Chernov code notes](https://people.cas.uab.edu/~mosya/cl/CPPcircle.html)).
    The circle results carry over to spheres in form [H].
- **Our numbers [E].** Stereo σ_z = z²σ_d/(fB) ([Intel formula, 26 Oct review](https://forums.intel.com/cipcp26785/attachments/cipcp26785/feeditem_no_topic/6363/1/BKM-For-Tuning-D435-and-D415-Cameras-Webinar_Rev3.pdf)):
  at z = 0.5 m, f = 600.2 px, B = 75.5 mm, σ_d = 0.15–0.3 px → σ_z ≈ 0.8–1.7 mm. A ball's image diameter is D·f/z:
  6.4 mm → 7.7 px, 9.5 mm → 11.4 px, 14 mm → 16.8 px, 19 mm → 22.8 px. Stereo fattens foreground edges
  ([MIT](https://people.csail.mit.edu/tfxue/project/multiview-stereo)), so 1–3 px of each ball's rim is unreliable [E];
  a 14 mm ball keeps ≈ 100–150 usable depth pixels per frame [E].
- **Fit for us [H].**
  - Detect in the object frame on fused points (many frames), not per frame: compact blobs off the main surface,
    connected to it by a thin post.
  - Fit centre and radius with a geometric least-squares fit started from Taubin/Hyper; fit only interior pixels
    (eroded mask). Then either keep the free radius, or pick the catalogue diameter with the best likelihood (BIC).
  - Optional check: colour silhouettes at full resolution (~34 px across for 14 mm) with a circle fit.
  - Falsifier (GT-free, zero scorings): the fitted radius of the same physical ball differs by > 1.5 mm between two
    episodes, or its across-frame bootstrap 90 % interval is wider than 3 mm.
  - Integrity note: the scan-derived 7 mm radius happens to equal the 14 mm catalogue size [R/D]. Choosing it *because*
    it matched the scan would still be a leak; only a pre-registered likelihood rule on our own points may pick it.

## 6. Provenance: ROI, tracks and frame without library_v3

- **Current residue [R].** `obs_all/*.npz` points were cropped with `roi = library_v3/<obj>.glb` (2 cm margin) (brief),
  and both public and eval tracks were seeded with library_v3 (`ops/jobs/eval_depth.sh` line 11: `MESH_DIR=$LIB`,
  `LIB=meshes/library_v3`).
- **GT-free ROI from the literature.** Visual hull from masks (dilated, voted) ∩ depth hull (seen free space) ∩
  above-table half-space; already in our toolbox as an envelope for screened Poisson
  ([Kazhdan et al. 2020](https://hhoppe.com/proj/envelope/); `lit/video-physics.md` §2.2) [R]. SymSeg uses the same
  occupancy-map idea [R]. This removes the mesh ROI entirely [H]. Falsifier: masks+depth ROI keeps < 95 % of the points
  the library ROI kept on public pairs, or adds > 5 % hand points (both GT-free).
- **Frame.** Three options, cheapest first:
  1. Keep the tracks and declare the frame convention inherited. The mesh is built only from video, but the frame
     (origin, axes) came from library_v3 through FoundationPose [R]. Honest flag needed.
  2. Re-track with a GT-free mesh (library_v5 or a GMG mesh from the same episode). FoundationPose model-based mode
     needs only a mesh [R] ([FoundationPose](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.08344v2)). GPU, per episode; the eval
     perception rerun cost ≈ $2.95 for 20 episodes [M] (brief).
  3. Model-free tracking: FoundationPose model-free (≈16 posed RGB-D reference views) or BundleSDF's own tracker, which
     needs only a first-frame mask [R] ([BundleSDF readme](https://github.com/NVlabs/BundleSDF)). Highest effort; frame
     then defined by the first frame.

## 8. Ranked suggestions for the GMG design (hypotheses; each needs the owner's go-ahead for GPU)

1. **Observed surface first (CPU).** Keep our fusion (TSDF / vote grid, thin-wall fixes from `lit/video-physics.md`)
   as the observed-surface source, with a mask+depth-hull ROI instead of the library_v3 ROI (§6). GT-free falsifier as
   in §6.
2. **Completion from SAM 3D with our metric pointmap, many frames (GPU, ≈$1–5 per split).** Per-frame meshes placed by
   their own metric layout and our `cam_T_obj` (H-SAM-PM, §3.1); then visibility-aware Sim(3) + per-axis scale against
   held-in frames (§3.4). Keep observed surface where observed; take generated surface only in unseen voxels (GenPC
   pattern) [H].
3. **MV-SAM3D with our poses and pointmaps** as the multi-view version of 2, if 2's per-frame meshes disagree
   (H-SAM-CONS fails) [H].
4. **Pixal3D-MV with a better view rule** (face-on, hand-masked, ≥3 views) as a second completion source in our frame
   [H] (`diag-2026-10-30/README.md`).
5. **Generic part detectors with catalogue priors**: marker balls (§5.2) and symmetry with free-space verification
   (§5.1). Both are object-agnostic and both protect against flips [H].
6. **Selection**: test R1–R4 (§4.4) on the existing 104 scores before building more selection machinery; keep wall and
   base decisions out of the ranker [H].
7. **Simulator copy**: watertight check after kit decode; CoACD colliders and a stable-pose check against the tracked
   rest pose (§1.5) [R/H].
