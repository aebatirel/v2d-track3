Why ep13 drops

On ep13 the cup's own AUC falls from 0.940 to 0.901. The rack's AUC also falls, from 0.993 to 0.972, although the rack mesh is the same. The scorer seems to tie objects in one episode together through a shared anchor [H]. On ep12, with the same two objects, the swap gains 0.033.
