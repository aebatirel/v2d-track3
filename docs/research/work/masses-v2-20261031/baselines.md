# Masses v2: baseline table (evidence for the selection)

Written 2026-10-31 (UTC), before any Masses v2 run. Generated by `scripts/baselines.py`, data in `baselines.json`; the
selection rule is `PREREG.md` §3.1. Every number is [M]: a PAST public-episode rollout score (registered ADD-AUC vs mocap,
library_v3 registration). Selecting on these is allowed, since they are public-episode scores and no eval episode is
involved. Each number was copied from its run's `job.log` RESULT line; the store copies were read once each, and the
RESULT timestamps are in `baselines.json`. Two exceptions: the first check's numbers come from its committed
`summary.json`, and the 23 Oct E-run numbers from the work log.

## What "baseline" means here
- **Learner and recipe.** The grasp-lock learner (`ops/jobs/glock_public.sh`): a 70,004,736-step FlashSAC fine-tune
  with FDR + VOC and the kp 400 embodiment.
- **Checkpoint choice.** The checkpoint is chosen on the mean sim_vs_ref of 3 evaluations, with no ground truth.
- **Scoring.** At least 5 official evaluations of that checkpoint, each scored vs mocap.
- **Conditions.** Our perceived reference, library_v3 meshes, 0.3 kg.
- **This is what `ops/jobs/mass_check.sh` trains** with REUSE_REF on a stored reference. The control arm M0 of an episode
  is therefore the baseline's condition, rerun with seeds 42 and 43.
- **Eligibility** (PREREG §3.1 step 2): current code (at or after 2c1e4dd, 24 Oct), CURVE_REP 3, library_v3 without
  carve pieces, and a stored reference in the layout mass_check.sh reuses.
- **One baseline per episode** (step 3): the frozen-recipe reference's run if one exists, else the most recent eligible
  run.
